\section{Method}
The searh began with these papers [citation needed] from earlier work by Odd Erik Gundersen, Thijs Snelleman and Peter Lawrence. 

Almost all papers that cited (or were cited by) these papers, were classed as "not relevant", "relevant" or "very relevant".

These factors were used to assess papers:
\begin{itemize}
\item In which context was this paper cited? If the context is one of the later categories, then it is relevant.
\item If the context is not relevant, then the cited paper is also not relevant. (e.g. a list of papers that form a dataset for auto reproducibility)
\item Was this paper only cited in the bibliography, and not the main text? Then it is not relevant.
\item Does the paper's abstract relate to any of the categories. Then it's relevant. If not, then not relevant.
\item Some papers are borderline. They are listed here [TODO: compile list]
\end{itemize}

"Very relevant" papers were then used to expand the search, by assessing their citations and citers.

\subsection{Categories}
Categories of papers were created and modified for relevant papers along the way.

\begin{itemize}
    \item Previous work \subitem Literature reviews or surveys of automatic reproducibility.
    \item Auto reproducibility \subitem Systems that automatically reproduce computational research.
    \item Research impact \subitem Contributes to our understanding of the impact of automated reproducibility.
    \item Terminology \subitem Contributes by defining terminology related to reproducibility.
    \item Validation \subitem Contributes benchmarks or other validation techniques for automatic reproducibility.
    \item Code Repair \subitem Systems that automatically repair old code.
    \item Journal policy \subitem Papers that describe policies at journals related to reproducibility.
    \item Reproducibility survey \subitem Papers that survey reproducibility in a field.
    \item Other  
\end{itemize}

Some whole categories were deemed not relevant only at the end of the process. They can be found in the appendix. [Citation needed]
